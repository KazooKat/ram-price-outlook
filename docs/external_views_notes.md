# External views (working notes, collected 2026-10-06)

Only for the "Outside forecasts" section. Verify against primary sources before citing; secondary aggregations marked.

## Primary or near-primary

- TrendForce, 2026-09-30 (https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260930-13258.html): 4Q26 contract prices PC DRAM +10-15% QoQ, server DRAM +10-15%, NAND +15-20%; consumer DRAM rising at a slower pace; client SSD increases restrained; "PC DRAM supply could decline in 2027 as more capacity shifts to server applications."
- TrendForce, 2026-07-30 (https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260730-13158.html): DRAM sufficiency ratio -1% to -2% in 2026, gap widens in 2027, new capacity delayed until 2028. NAND sufficiency ratio turns positive in 2027 -> downward NAND price pressure.
- TrendForce, 2026-07-09: server DRAM contract +13-18% QoQ in 3Q26; long-term agreements cap increases.
- Micron FQ4 2026 call, 2026-09-30 (secondary transcript https://www.tradingkey.com/news/transcripts/262195863-tradingkey; techpowerup https://www.techpowerup.com/353296/): supply "much tighter in 2027 and 2028 than 2026"; no line of sight to balance; Idaho first wafers mid-CY2027, meaningful supply several quarters later; Singapore cleanroom 2H CY2028; industry DRAM bit shipments low-20s % growth in CY2027 and CY2028; >75% of 2027 output committed. Micron exited Crucial consumer brand (Tom's Hardware).
- SK hynix CEO Kwak Noh-jung, Aug 2026 (secondary: sammyfans): shortage could last until end of 2030. Yongin fab 1 cleanroom pulled to Feb 2027; M15X producing DRAM.
- Gartner (CRN AU https://www.crn.com.au/news/2026/hardware/gartner-memory-prices-will-stay-crazy-through-to-at-least): DRAM 3.6% undersupply through 2027, prices +2% in 2027; DRAM+SSD prices +130% by end of 2026; PC prices +17%; shortage until 2H27.
- Kye-hyun Kyung (former Samsung DS head), NAEK forum Seoul, May 2026 (androidheadlines 2026-05-19): "significant drop in memory prices by the second half of next year" (2H27), driven by Chinese capacity (CXMT) and risk of AI capex pullback.
- Acer CEO Jason Chen (Yahoo Finance): price hikes could begin to reverse by late 2027.
